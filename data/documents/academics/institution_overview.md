# Institution Overview — Dr. N.G.P. Institute of Technology (NGPiTECH)

## Identity
- Full name: Dr. N.G.P. Institute of Technology (NGPiTECH)
- Location: Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India
- Type: Autonomous engineering institution (autonomous status conferred by UGC from academic year 2022–2023)
- Tagline: "Empowering Engineers, Nurturing Citizenship"
- Placement track record advertised: 90%+ / 95% (see Programmes document for detail)

## Accreditation & Statutory Status (confirmed via multiple NGPiTECH department pages)
- Established: 2007.
- Affiliated to Anna University.
- Approved by AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education).
- Recognized by UGC under Section 2(f).
- Accredited by NAAC and NBA.
- Autonomous status conferred by UGC from academic year 2022–2023.
- Programme count: 10 UG programmes — B.E. in Biomedical Engineering, CSE, CSE (Cyber Security), Civil Engineering, ECE, EEE, Mechanical Engineering; B.Tech in Information Technology, Computer Science & Business Systems (CSBS), and AI & Data Science. Plus 4 PG programmes (M.E. CSE, M.E. Embedded System Technologies, M.E. Power Electronics & Drives, MBA) and Ph.D./research programmes in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Computer Science, Civil, and IT.
