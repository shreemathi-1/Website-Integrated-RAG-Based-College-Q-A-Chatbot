# Department of Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) — NGPiTECH

## About
CSBS is a 4-year interdisciplinary UG programme established in 2022 with an intake of 60, combining core computer science topics with business analytics, humanities, and management discipline. It targets "Business 5.0" readiness — technical + organizational/business perspective.

## Industry Partnerships / MoUs
Oracle Workforce Development Program Centre, Cisco Networking Academy, RedHat Academy, IBM Centre of Excellence, EMC² Academic Alliance, AOSTA Technology Incubation Centre, Lakshya Cyber Security Centre.

## Vision (implied from mission statements)
To equip students with state-of-the-art contemporary technology in Computer Science and basic engineering science, combined with business principles.

## Mission
- Impart contemporary technology aligned with industry and society's needs.
- Educate students in state-of-the-art technologies, developing analytical and design skills.
- Prepare students for higher education and research.
- Enable successful careers in CSBS meeting evolving stakeholder needs.

## Facilities
The CSBS Lab features a high-performance computer setup described as one of the most powerful in the higher-education sector, used for industry-style learning experiences.

## Career / Internship Model
Students get full-time internship and on-the-job training opportunities at companies like Amazon, Capgemini, Bosch, Cognizant, Flipkart, Hexaware, IBM, Infineon, ITC, and Wipro — including a pre-placement-offer engagement model (internship of 6 months to a year running alongside academics, converting to full-time employment based on performance).
