# Department of Civil Engineering — NGPiTECH

## About
The department has existed since 2011, with 8 batches of graduates as of recent record. It has grown into a fully-fledged department with seven well-equipped labs across different civil engineering streams. It maintains a "Worksite of Tomorrow Lab" to help students engage with technology changes across the project/asset lifecycle.

## Faculty & Research
- 9 faculty members, 50% holding doctorates, specializing in Structural Engineering and Environmental Engineering.
- Total grants of about Rs. 10.35 lakhs received from ISRO, AICTE, TNSCST, IE(I), DST (SERB), and CSIR for technical events and research development.

## Consultancy Services
Total Station Surveying, Concrete sample testing, Sand Grading, SPT for various strata, Non-Destructive Tests (Rebound Hammer Test, Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity Test).

## Professional Society Memberships
Indian Concrete Institute, Association of Consulting Civil Engineers (India), Indian Geotechnical Society (IGS — student chapter inaugurated 2018), Indian Green Building Council (Hyderabad), Indian Water Works Association, The Institution of Engineers (India), Indian Plumbing Association, Builders' Association of India, Indian Society for Technical Education.

## Notable Events
- International Conference on Smart Materials and Techniques for Sustainable Development (2019).
- Second International Conference on Sustainable Design, Materials and Manufacturing in Engineering (ISDMME-2023), held April 5–6, 2023.
- More than 180 technical events (seminars, conferences, FDPs, trainings) conducted for internal and external participants.
