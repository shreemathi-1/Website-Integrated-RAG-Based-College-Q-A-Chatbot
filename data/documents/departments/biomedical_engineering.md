# Department of Biomedical Engineering — NGPiTECH

## About
Established in 2012. Students receive hands-on clinical training at Kovai Medical Center and Hospital (KMCH), a multi-disciplinary super-specialty hospital with a wide range of state-of-the-art medical equipment, giving broad exposure across hospital departments.

## Laboratories
- Biochemistry & Human Physiology Laboratory
- Pathology and Microbiology Laboratory
- Biomedical Instrumentation Laboratory
- Digital Signal Processing Laboratory
- Diagnostic and Therapeutic Equipment Laboratory

## Industry / Clinical Links
Regular interactions with Kovai Medical Centre and Hospital and biomedical companies for training, internships, projects, and research in emerging biomedical fields.

## Professional Society
Society for Engineering Education Enrichment (SEEE) — a nonprofit founded in 2016 by young graduate engineers in India, supporting professional needs across engineering disciplines through structured programmes.
