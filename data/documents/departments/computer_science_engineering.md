# Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) — NGPiTECH

## About
Established in 2007. Covers computation, programming languages, program design, computer hardware/software, spanning research into computational processes and new computing systems. The department has grown into a "centre of excellence" with well-equipped computing infrastructure.

## Vision
To produce technically competent Computer Science and Engineering professionals to meet the technological needs of society.

## Mission
- Impart technical skills in CSE through innovative teaching-learning methods.
- Collaborate with corporates, research organizations, and professional societies.

## Industry Partnerships / MoUs
Oracle Workforce Development Program Centre, Cisco Networking Academy, IBM Centre of Excellence, EMC² Academic Alliance, AOSTA Technology Incubation Center, Lakshya Cyber Security Center.

## Industry Labs
Oracle, RedHat, Amazon Web Services, and AOSTA labs — directly managed by the respective companies, giving students real-time exposure.

## AOSTA Technology Incubation Center
CSE signed an MoU with Aosta Software Technologies India Limited (Coimbatore) to establish the Dr.NGPIT–Aosta Technology Incubation Center. It supports student innovation projects (mobile apps, medical equipment integration, industrial training, prototype development, hardware/software support, mentoring) and can execute/deliver projects for internal use or external clients.

## Institution Accreditation Context (as stated on CSE dept page)
NGP IT was established in 2007; affiliated to Anna University; approved by AICTE; recognized by UGC under Section 2(f); accredited by NAAC and NBA.
