# Department of Information Technology — NGPiTECH

## About
Established in 2007, the Department of Information Technology offers a B.Tech in Information Technology. The programme covers programming, database management, computer networks, cybersecurity, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and data analytics. Faculty are engaged in research in AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Analytics, Data Mining, Cognitive Radio, Cloud Computing, and Image Processing.

## Key Stats
- 95% placement success rate.
- 15+ active industry MoUs.
- 75% of students undergo internships with a stipend.
- 1,000+ student participations in hackathons and technical events.
- 1,000+ online and industrial certifications completed by students.

## Vision
To produce graduates for IT industries who have a strong mind and attitude to develop solutions based on innovations and social cause with continued learning.

## Mission
- Empower students with cutting-edge technical skills and a problem-solving attitude.
- Foster innovation and social responsibility to drive positive change through technology.
- Collaborate with industry partners for real-world experience.
- Provide a learning environment that encourages continuous growth and adaptability.

## Laboratories
- Project and Research Lab — dedicated to research, collaborative projects, and innovation.
- Programming Lab VI — coding sessions and software development.
- Programming Lab VII — advanced programming and algorithmic design.
- Programming Lab XI — collaborative engineering, application development and testing.

## Industry Presence & Partnerships
Industry-inside-campus partners: Nunnari Labs, Pinesphere Solutions Pvt. Ltd, Avenza Consulting Services, Inno Valley Works.

Other industry linkages: Global Knowledge Technologies, Krishtec, Vishaithalam, OneData Software Solutions, Hackup Technologies.

Notable alumni employers: Turing, Quinbay Technologies, Codingmart Technologies, Odyssey Technologies, Rently Software Development, Amazon, ZOHO, Tech Mahindra, CTS, TCS, AtoS-Syntel.

## Professional Society Student Chapters (hosted by IT dept, open institution-wide)
- **ACM (Association for Computing Machinery)** — chapter instituted October 2013.
- **CSI (Computer Society of India)** — established 1965 (parent body); facilitates research, knowledge-sharing, and career development for CS/IT professionals.
- **IEEE Student Branch** — inaugurated July 2010; runs Project Design Contests, Technical Quizzes, seminars; Best Student Branch award holder since 2010; Women in Engineering (WIE) affinity group active.
- **ISTE (Indian Society for Technical Education)** — workshops, seminars, and competitions.
- **ITACT** — focuses on cloud computing / IaaS topics.

## Curriculum Overview (topics covered)
Programming & Problem Solving, Mathematical Foundations, Core Computer Science, Systems & Networking, AI & Emerging Technologies, Modern Development Skills, Software Engineering, Hands-on Learning, Industry Exposure, Capstone Experience. Emerging tech areas: AI, Data Analytics, Cloud Computing, IoT, Blockchain, Full Stack Development.
