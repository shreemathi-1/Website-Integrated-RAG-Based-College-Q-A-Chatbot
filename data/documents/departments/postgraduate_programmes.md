# Postgraduate (PG) Programmes — NGPiTECH

NGPiTECH offers 4 PG programmes: M.E. Computer Science and Engineering, M.E. Embedded System Technologies, M.E. Power Electronics & Drives, and MBA.

## M.E. Embedded System Technologies
The "Embedded Control" track addresses designing electronics/embedded systems closely integrated with mechanical and control systems. Provides deep, focused postgraduate training in embedded systems design.
Source: https://www.drngpit.ac.in/study/postgraduate/embedded-system-technology

## M.E. Computer Science and Engineering
Covers advanced topics: algorithms, distributed systems, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, cybersecurity, and software engineering. Emphasizes research-oriented learning, innovation, and problem-solving for careers in R&D, academia, high-end software development, and emerging tech domains.
Source: https://www.drngpit.ac.in/study/postgraduate/com-sci-engineering

## MBA (Master of Business Administration)
A postgraduate degree developing skills for business/management careers, preparing students for leadership roles. Covers finance, marketing, human resources, operations, and strategy, alongside decision-making, problem-solving, communication, and leadership skills. Includes case studies, internships, group projects, and industry interactions.
Source: https://www.drngpit.ac.in/study/postgraduate/mba

## Shared PG Internship/Placement Model (institution-wide)
Full-time internship and on-the-job training opportunities at Amazon, Capgemini, Bosch, Cognizant, Flipkart, Hexaware, IBM, Infineon, ITC, Wipro, etc. Pre-placement-offer engagement model: 6-month to 1-year internship running alongside academics, converting to full-time employment based on performance.
