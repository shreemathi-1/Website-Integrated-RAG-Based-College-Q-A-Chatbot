# Department of Mechanical Engineering — NGPiTECH

## About
Covers design, development, construction, and testing of mechanical and thermal devices — tools, engines, and machines. The department is recognized as a research centre by Anna University and conducts research in composites, machining, design, nanotechnology, and coatings.

## Faculty
13 faculty members — 6 hold Ph.Ds and 8 are pursuing doctoral programmes.

## Student Organizations
- **Indian Welding Society (IWS)** — student forum started in academic year 2017–2018 to facilitate exchange of ideas on welding through lectures, workshops, and seminars.
- **MECHLADOZ** — a student-run organization (since 2010) aimed at enriching students with well-rounded professional and personal qualities.

## Activities
Frequent industry visits, subject-related seminars, guest lectures, and workshops to deepen student understanding of mechanical engineering topics.
