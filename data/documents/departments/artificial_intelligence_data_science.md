# Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI&DS) — NGPiTECH

## About
The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science delivers education in machine learning, deep learning, data analytics, big data, LLMs, and intelligent systems, balancing theory with practical application through projects, internships, and industry collaboration.

## Key Stats
- 100% placement success.
- 75% of students get paid internships.
- 20+ active industry MoUs.
- 1,000+ wins in hackathons and technical competitions.
- 100% of projects lead to research publications.

## Vision
To produce technically competent artificial intelligence and data science professionals with social responsibility.

## Mission
- Impart technical skills in AI and data science through progressive learning practices.
- Collaborate with corporates, research organizations, and professional societies for R&D.
- Establish modern computing facilities and industry-supported centers.
- Inculcate ethical values and social responsibility among students.

## Laboratories
- Programming Lab VI — advanced computing paradigms and software development.
- Programming Lab IX — high-end workstations for algorithmic design and data science.
- Industry-supported centers: Oracle Academy, ICTACT-EM2, Red Hat Academy, AOSTA Incubation Center, National Cyber Defence Research Centre (NCDRC) on AI, Cyber Security Center.

## Industry Partnerships / MoUs
Oracle Academy, Cisco Networking Academy, AOSTA Software, Lakshya Cyber Security Labs, National Cyber Safety and Security Standards.

## Curriculum Overview
Programming & Problem Solving, Mathematical Foundations, Core Computer Science, Systems & Networking, AI & Emerging Technologies, Modern Development Skills, Software Engineering, Hands-on Learning, Industry Exposure, Capstone Experience.
