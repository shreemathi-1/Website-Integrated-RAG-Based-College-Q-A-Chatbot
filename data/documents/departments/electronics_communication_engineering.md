# Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) — NGPiTECH

## About
Established in 2007. Prepares engineers and researchers through engagement in research spanning computational processes, new computing system design, and applications. The curriculum blends a computer science core with an array of technical electives, and an industry-aligned focus on Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cyber Security, and Cloud Computing.

## Key Stats
- 100% placement support.
- 75% paid internship opportunities.
- 1,000+ achievements in technical events.
- 100% project-to-publication conversion.

## Vision
To produce technically competent Electronics and Communication Engineering professionals to meet the technological needs of society, with profound technical knowledge and contemporary education for a successful career.

## Industry Partnerships / MoUs
Oracle Workforce Development Program Centre, Cisco Networking Academy, IBM Centre of Excellence, EMC² Academic Alliance, AOSTA Technology Incubation Center, Lakshya Cyber Security Center.

## Notable Alumni Outcomes
Includes graduates pursuing an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Windsor (Canada) and working as a Design Engineer at Tessolve Semiconductor Pvt Ltd (Bangalore).
