# Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) — NGPiTECH

## About
EEE offers industry-focused specializations aligned with modern energy trends — power electronics, control systems, power systems, and electric vehicles — helping students build expertise in smart and sustainable technologies.

## Teaching Practices
Lectures, ICT-enabled instruction, project-based learning, hands-on laboratory work, and tools such as MATLAB/Simulink, to foster experiential learning and industry readiness.

## Program Specific Outcomes (selected)
- PSO2: Develop electronic circuits, embedded systems, and automation solutions for EV and robotics applications.
- PSO3: Create sustainable solutions using modern tools and emerging technologies like IoT, renewable energy systems, and smart grids.

## Industry Academic Interface (IAI)
Acts as a bridge for institute-industry engagement — internships, industry projects, seminars, workshops, and student exchange programs. MoUs signed with TESSOLVE, RRT Electro Power (P) Limited, SIEMENS, and National Instruments for training and placement.

## International Collaboration
The department runs research internship programs for undergraduates (via CIR) with MoUs held with several international universities (specific university names not listed on the fetched page — verify with the department directly).
