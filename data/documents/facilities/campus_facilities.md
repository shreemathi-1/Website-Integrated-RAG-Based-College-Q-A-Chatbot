# Campus Facilities — Dr. N.G.P. Institute of Technology (NGPiTECH)

## Central Library
Source: https://www.drngpit.ac.in/academic-facilities/library

Established in 2007, the Dr. N.G.P. Institute of Technology Library and Information Center operates on the motto "Books are for use and not for preservation."

**Book Collection**
| Library | No. of Volumes | No. of Titles |
|---|---|---|
| Central Library | 42,575 | 10,023 |
| Department Library | 7,288 | 4,566 |

**Online Journal Subscriptions**
- IEEE All Society Periodicals
- DELNET
- NDLI (National Digital Library)
- VIDWAN (IRINS) Portal
- Elsevier Science Direct
- EBSCO Business Source Elite

**General Rules**
- Users must scan their ID card at the E-Gate Register scanner when entering/leaving.
- ID card must be carried at all times inside the library.
- Books are issued only against a barcoded ID card.
- Strict silence must be maintained.
- Personal belongings (bags, files, umbrellas, etc.) are not allowed inside; must be left at the entrance rack.
- Open Access System: books/journals taken from racks should be left on tables, not re-shelved by users.

**Book Circulation Rules**
- Return period: 14 days (students), 2 months (non-teaching/admin staff), 4 months (teaching staff).
- Borrowing limit: 6 books (UG/PG students), 8 books (non-teaching/admin staff), 10 books (teaching staff).
- Returned books are re-issued only from the next day afternoon onward.
- Late return fine: Rs. 2 per day.
- Lost book: must be reported immediately; must be replaced within 15 days of the due date, or the borrower pays double the book's cost.
- Reference books, CDs, DVDs, project reports, back volumes, and periodicals are for in-library reference only — not issued for borrowing.
- Photocopying of individual periodical articles is allowed; photocopying a full book is not permitted.

## IT Infrastructure
Source: https://www.drngpit.ac.in/academic-facilities/it-infrastructure

- 15 academic and 5 industry-driven computer laboratories.
- Desktops from Acer, Dell, HCL, HP, IBM, and Zenith.
- Campus LAN via Optic-Fibre and CAT6 cabling, 10G uplink (scalable to 100G).
- 1 Gbps dedicated 1:1 leased-line internet from Tata Teleservices Limited.
- Wi-Fi 6 access points (Quantum, Cambium) under secure cloud management.
- Industry-driven labs: Texas Instruments, National Instruments LabView Academy, Cisco Networking Academy, Oracle Workforce Development Program, Cybersecurity Center.
- Microsoft Open Value Education Subscription for licensed software.
- Dedicated Open-Source Lab and standalone Language Lab (Clarity Learning tools).
- AOSTA Incubation Centre bridging industry practice with classroom learning.
- Security: Sophos Firewall, K7 Enterprise Endpoint Security, 24/7 CCTV across academic blocks and labs.
- Power backup: 253 KVA online UPS units, 380 KVA primary + 250 KVA standby generators.
- 10-member technical Helpdesk team for network/computing support.

## Campus Amenities
Source: https://www.drngpit.ac.in/academic-facilities/amenities

- **Conference Center / Auditorium**: Centrally air-conditioned, seating capacity 1,200, built on 1,000 sq. m, with hands-free LCD projector and audio-visual equipment for seminars, workshops, and symposiums.
- **Transport**: College-operated transport for students commuting from across Coimbatore and surrounding areas.
- **Hospital**: Kovai Medical Center and Hospital (KMCH) — a 1,000-bed multi-disciplinary super-specialty hospital, well connected by road, rail, and air.
- **Banking**: Indian Bank (Kalapatti branch) with an on-campus ATM.
- **Cafeteria**: One cafeteria and two canteens offering subsidized, nutritious food.
- **Security**: 24-hour security at boys' and girls' hostels.
