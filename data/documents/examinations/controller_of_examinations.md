# Controller of Examinations — Dr. N.G.P. Institute of Technology (NGPiTECH)

## About
Dr. N.G.P. Institute of Technology was conferred Autonomous Status by the University Grants Commission (UGC) from the academic year 2022–2023. The Office of the Controller of Examinations (COE) was established subsequently to manage all examination-related activities for UG and PG programmes.

## Responsibilities of the Controller of Examinations
1. **Scheduling & Timetable** — Preparing and publishing the examination schedule, including internal assessments and semester-end examinations, in coordination with Anna University.
2. **Hall Ticket Issuance** — Generating and distributing hall tickets to eligible students after verifying attendance and academic requirements.
3. **Exam Coordination** — Coordinating with departments to appoint invigilators, arrange examination halls, and ensure smooth conduct of examinations.
4. **Malpractice Control** — Enforcing anti-malpractice measures and handling malpractice cases per Anna University regulations and institutional norms.
5. **Result Processing** — Overseeing collection, evaluation, and tabulation of answer scripts, and publishing results within the stipulated timeframe.
6. **Regulations Compliance** — Ensuring all examination activities comply with AICTE, UGC, and Anna University examination regulations and circulars.
7. **Grievance Redressal** — Addressing student grievances related to results, revaluation requests, and exam-related issues.
8. **Record Maintenance** — Maintaining academic records, mark statements, transcripts, and other official documents for all students.
